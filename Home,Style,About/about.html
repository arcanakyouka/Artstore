About the Artist

Arcana Kyouka • Illustrations • Storytelling

Where myth, memory, and dream collide.

Arcana Kyouka is an independent artist focused on fantasy illustration, story-rich worlds, and collectible art prints inspired by mythology, folklore, and surreal imagination.

The work explores strange beauty, forgotten rituals, and dreamlike landscapes where ordinary objects carry hidden meaning. Each piece is designed to feel like a fragment of a larger story.

From character portraits to atmospheric scenes, the goal is to build a visual archive of things that feel uncanny, luminous, and deeply personal.

Mediums Digital illustration, fantasy portraiture, concept art, and collectible prints.
Influences Mythology, folklore, anime, games, and atmospheric art traditions.
Theme Dreamlike scenes and symbolic stories told through luminous, eerie imagery.
