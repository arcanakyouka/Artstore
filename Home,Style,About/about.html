About Me

arcanaKyouka • Illustrator • Writer

A Little World of my Own

Hi, I'm the artist behind arcanaKyouka, I'm an independent artist and writer, focused on creating art prints ranging from nature, characters, fantasy, and things I want to draw.

I enjoy taking photos of animals and nature in my spare time. I'm also really into anime and japanese culture. These interests drive my ideas and creative process. I want my artwork to give people a sense of wonder and curiosity. I also want people to look at my work and get inspired; art gives us a way to create stories, explore ideas, and imaginenew ideas.

My name is Japanese and comes from "arcana" (mystery) and "kyouka" (mirror flower). This idea is also how I view art. Everyone sees art differently, and art reflects what people see back to them. Just like the optical illusion with a bunny and a duck, you might see one before the other and might not see the other at all. Art is subject to opinions and ideas, but remember the artist might have a different idea. So dive deeper and try to understand what your looking at.

Mediums Digital illustration, fantasy portraiture, concept art, and collectible prints.
Influences Mythology, folklore, anime, games, and atmospheric art traditions.
Theme Dreamlike scenes and symbolic stories told through luminous, eerie imagery.
