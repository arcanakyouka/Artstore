Lantern Keeper

Lantern Keeper imagines a traveler responsible for carrying a small light through uncertain places. The character is less a hero than a guide, defined by patience and attention.

The work draws from folktales about wayfinders, night watchmen, and the people who make difficult roads feel possible.

Back to Gallery

Inspiration

Folklore, night journeys, hand-held lights, and the guardians who help others find their way.

Materials

Digital character concept with layered shadow, warm light, and a weathered storybook texture.

Collection

A character study from the ArcanaKyouka gallery archive, built around quiet acts of care.
