Moth Valley

Moth Valley is an imagined environment where the landscape seems to breathe after dusk. The scene balances open distance with the intimate pull of a small, hidden place.

Its atmosphere is inspired by nocturnal gardens, mountain paths, and the strange comfort of finding a familiar light in a wild landscape.
