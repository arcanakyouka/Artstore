Glass Rose

A crystalline bloom preserved beneath moonlit glass, surrounded by a halo of faint sigils and an old-world hush. This piece evokes memory, fragility, and the quiet magic held inside forgotten things.

Part of the ArcanaKyouka gallery archive, this work explores the beauty of keeping something fleeting just beyond reach.
